Product Concept

Meet your everyday bite.

GAINROOT is a baked snack concept built around orange sweet potato. We are developing small, convenient bites with a planned five-piece pack—easy to bring into everyday routines.

Baked Texture Delicate crumb & natural color

Sweet potato at the center

Orange sweet-potato powder is the signature base of the current recipe. It gives each bite its characteristic natural golden color and warm earthy profile.

A baked snack concept

Small bites developed through recipe and baking tests to achieve an enjoyable, firm-yet-tender bite that travels cleanly without crumbling.

Five bites per planned pack

A simple pack format that is still being refined—calibrated to offer a practical, satisfying portion that fits neatly into pockets and bags.
